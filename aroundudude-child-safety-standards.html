Child Safety Standards

AroundUDude – Meet. Connect. Chat.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

AroundUDude does not tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any behaviour that sexually exploits, endangers, or targets children.

Users must not use AroundUDude to create, request, upload, share, distribute, promote, or facilitate child sexual abuse or exploitation. We may take action against accounts that violate these standards, subject to applicable law and our review process.

2. How to Report a User

If you encounter a user who appears to be engaging in child exploitation, grooming, harassment, or other prohibited conduct, report that user from the chat screen:

  1. Open the conversation with the user.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the chat screen.
  3. Select Report User.
  4. Submit the report. AroundUDude records the reporting account, reported account, chat room identifier, and report time in its reporting system.

After reporting, the reporting user is taken out of the chat. Reports are intended to be reviewed by the AroundUDude team and may lead to appropriate account or safety action.

3. Blocking a User

You can use the app's Block User feature to avoid unwanted interactions. Blocking is separate from reporting and does not send a safety report by itself. If you believe a user is violating these standards, please use Report User as well.

4. Review and Enforcement

AroundUDude reviews reports through its reporting process. Depending on the information available and the seriousness of the concern, appropriate action may include restricting or disabling an account, preventing further interactions, and taking other steps permitted by our policies and applicable law.

We will cooperate with relevant authorities and respond to valid legal requests. Suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation will be escalated to the appropriate authorities or designated reporting organisations where required by applicable law.

5. Contact for Child Safety Concerns

For child-safety concerns or questions about these standards, contact:

Please include relevant account details and a description of the concern. Do not email, download, or forward suspected illegal sexual imagery involving a child.

6. Safety and Legal Compliance

AroundUDude is committed to maintaining a safer user environment and complying with applicable child-safety laws. We may update these standards as our app, safety procedures, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be available on this page.
