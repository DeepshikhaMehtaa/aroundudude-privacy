1. Delete Your Account in the App

You can request deletion of your AroundUDude account directly from the app. The Delete Account option is available in the side navigation drawer (menu), not on the Profile screen.

  1. Open AroundUDude and log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the side navigation drawer using the menu icon.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the confirmation message and confirm your request.

2. Request Deletion Without Accessing the App

If you cannot access the app, email us from the email address registered with your AroundUDude account. Include your registered email address or user ID and use “AroundUDude Account Deletion Request” as the subject.

Email an Account Deletion Request
Verification: We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password or one-time passcodes by email.

3. What Data Is Deleted

When a verified deletion request is completed, we will delete the account and associated personal data our systems are designed to remove, which may include:

  • Account information, such as registered email address and account identifier.
  • Profile information, such as nickname, age, gender and interests.
  • Chat messages and other user-generated content associated with the account, where applicable and technically supported.
  • Other account-related records that are not required to be retained.

4. Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by applicable law, to address security or fraud concerns, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Any retained data will be limited to the relevant purpose and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Before publishing: Confirm that your deletion process actually removes the listed data from Firebase Authentication, Firestore or Realtime Database, Storage, and any other systems you use. Update this page if some data is not deleted or if specific retention periods apply. Do not claim deletion of data your app retains.

5. Guest Profiles

If you use AroundUDude as a guest without registering, you can contact us at the email address below to request deletion of saved guest profile information. Include details that can help us identify the guest profile. We may request additional information to verify the request.
