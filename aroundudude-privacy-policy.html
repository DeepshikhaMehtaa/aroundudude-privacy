1. Introduction

Welcome to AroundUDude. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the AroundUDude mobile application (“App”).

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Profile information: nickname, age, gender, and interests that you choose to provide.
  • Account information: email address and authentication information if you choose to register or log in.
  • Chat information: text messages exchanged with other users.
  • Technical information: information such as device, app, diagnostic, and usage data where it is generated or processed to operate, maintain, and secure the App.

AroundUDude does not request or collect your precise location.

3. Guest Access

You can use guest access without creating an account. Information you enter in a guest profile may be saved so that your profile is available when you close and reopen the App. You may choose to register or log in when those options are available.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • create and maintain user profiles;
  • help users discover profiles and interests;
  • deliver text messages between users;
  • preserve guest profile information between app sessions;
  • provide registration and login features;
  • operate, maintain, and secure the App; and
  • identify technical issues and improve the user experience.

5. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate, maintain, and secure the App. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, our rights, or the security of the App.

Profile information may be visible to other users as part of the App’s profile-discovery features. Chat messages are intended for the users participating in the relevant conversation.

6. Chat and User Safety

Only send information in chats that you are comfortable sharing with the recipient. Avoid sharing passwords, payment-card details, financial information, or other sensitive information. Other users may copy or save information you share with them.

7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, meet applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the App and its users.

To request deletion of your account or personal information, contact us using the email address below. Guest users may also contact us to request deletion of saved guest profile information. We may need to retain certain information where required or permitted by law.

9. Children's Privacy

AroundUDude is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the App under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You can contact us to make a privacy-related request.
